About this map

A small, live, hand-drawn Ohio State

Small OSU is an illustrated, isometric map of the Ohio State campus in Columbus that you can drag, zoom and turn. The buses on it are the real ones, the sky is today's sky, and the people and cars are a gentle simulation of a campus going about its day.

Unofficial. Small OSU is a fan project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by The Ohio State University, and it uses none of the university's logos or marks. For official maps, bus times and campus information, use the university's own sites.

How it's made

  1. OpenStreetMap geometry. Every building, street, path, lamp post and patch of grass comes from OpenStreetMap, the map the world edits together, and so do most of the trees. A small build script turns it into one compact file of footprints, heights, roof shapes and road widths, and plants generated trees in woods and leafy back gardens where the map draws none.
  2. An illustration, drawn in your browser. Nothing here is a pre-rendered picture. The page projects that geometry into an isometric view (a camera tilted 30° down, in four directions) and paints it with a warm, hand-picked palette: brick and limestone walls, lit windows, soft shadows, the season's trees.
  3. Tiles in the background. The map is cut into tiles that are painted by web workers on separate threads, so dragging stays smooth while new streets are drawn. Buses and people are drawn on top each frame, and buildings that stand in front of them are redrawn so they pass behind.
  4. Live buses. Campus bus positions, routes and stops come from the Ohio State CABS feed every few seconds. Evenings and weekends only a route or two run, so a quiet map is often an honest one.
  5. The real sky. Temperature, cloud, rain and snow come from Open-Meteo, and the light follows the sun over Columbus: golden hours, dusk, lit windows at night.
  6. Simulated life. Cars and students are not tracked. They are a simulation that walks the real paths and drives the real streets, busier between classes and quieter at night.
